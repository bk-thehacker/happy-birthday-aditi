Meri Sabse Pyari Aditi (Meri Laado, My Darling),

Is khoobsurat din par, main tumhe batana chahta hoon ki tum mere liye kitni special ho. Jab se tum meri life mein aayi ho, har ek din khoobsurat, pyara aur khushiyon se bhar gaya hai. Tumhari hassi mere din ki sabse badi khushi hai, aur tumhari aakhon mein jo apnapan hai woh mujhe har waqt sukoon deta hai.

Tum sirf meri girlfriend nahi ho Aditi — tum meri sabse acchi dost ho, mera safe zone ho, aur Arjun ki favorite person ho jiske saath main apne saare sapne dekhta hoon. Tumhari har ek mirror selfie, tumhari pyaari baatein aur tumhara accha dil mujhe yaad dilaata hai ki Arjun kitna lucky hai jo use tum mili ho.

Khuda kare yeh naya saal tumhari life mein utni hi khushiyan, kamyabi aur hassi laye jitni tum meri life mein roz laati ho. Main promise karta hoon ki hamesha tumhare saath khada rahunga, tumhari pyari si smile ki hamesha hifazat karunga aur zindagi ke har mausam mein tumse behad pyaar karunga.

Dil Ki Gehraiyon Se, Hamesha Tumhara, Arjun ❤️
